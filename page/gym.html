Gym machines and lifts

The same model, run on the machines you asked about. One rule explains almost all of it. A machine that pushes on your shin (open chain) loads the kneecap most near a straight knee. A movement where your foot is on the floor or a plate (closed chain) loads it most when you're deeply bent. So limit leg extensions at the top of their range, and limit squats, presses and lunges at the bottom.
