Six home exercises, and what each one changes

They're ordered from least to most kneecap load. The readout under each figure gives the model's outer-facet stress, and all of them sit well below the ~11 MPa a running stride puts on the same spot. That your pain has dropped since you started working out is the best sign there is: it means the knee is responding to load. Keep these going right up to race week. Doses are under each figure.

How to dose it. Mild discomfort up to about 3/10 during an exercise is fine, as long as it settles within 24 hours and isn't worse the next morning. If it is, drop the load or depth for the next session rather than stopping. Start every exercise at the easy end.
