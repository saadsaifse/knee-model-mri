Inside one running stride

Here's a running stride on a treadmill, with your left knee's anatomy underneath following each moment. Use the slider to stop on any stage. The ones to look at are loading (just after the foot lands), where the tendons and the outer facet turn red, and swing, where the knee folds deep but carries almost nothing. Switch to Walking to see the opposite: little load, but the kneecap riding furthest outward in the shallow groove.
