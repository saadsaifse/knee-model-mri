New in this version: the real anatomy under every figure. Each figure now has two anatomical views of your left knee that move with that figure's knee angle. Side view (from the outer side, front of the knee to the right): femur, kneecap, quadriceps and patellar tendons, tibia, fibula, meniscus and the outer collateral ligament (LCL), with the cruciate ligaments (ACL, PCL) drawn faintly because they sit inside the joint. The small Baker's cyst sits behind the knee. From below (the "sunrise" view radiologists use): the kneecap sitting in its groove, sliding outward by the model's shift, held on the inner side by the MPFL and on the outer side by the retinaculum. Blue is cartilage. Red shows load: on the tendons it scales with tendon force, and on the kneecap's cartilage with stress on the outer facet, where the small crack (the red tick) is. Drawings are schematic, not traced from your scan.
